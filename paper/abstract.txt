Beyond the Ford-circle law: parabolic renormalization and the shape of Mandelbrot satellite bulbs

To first order in $1/q$, the satellite bulb of the Mandelbrot set at internal angle $p/q$ is a round disc of diameter $2\sin(\pi p/q)q^{-2}$: the Ford-circle law. We study the first correction. In the bulb-scale variable $u=q^2\varepsilon$, the first coefficient of the cycle multiplier that depends on $p/q$ is $\kappa(p/q)=(\iota_{p/q}-1/2)/q$, where $\iota_{p/q}$ is the holomorphic index of the parabolic point; we prove this, identify $\kappa+1/2$ with the index of a rotation quotient of the normal form, and obtain an $O(q^2)$ recursion that certifies $\kappa(1/q)$ up to $q=32768$. Numerically, the correction is governed by parabolic renormalization. The limit of $\kappa(1/q)$ is the horn-map index $\kappa_0=0.0238258874\ldots-0.0523046591\ldots i$ of $z+z^2$, to 24 digits. Along $q=pN+r$ the limits of $\kappa$ and of the bulb diameter follow the horn germ times the Inou-Shishikura phase, and an exact Moebius form of that phase accounts for the entire $O(1/q)$ correction. Iterating along large partial quotients gives constants that converge geometrically, with ratio $0.042$, to a constant $\kappa_*$ of renormalization composed with complex conjugation; its fixed point differs from that of Lanford and Yampolsky. At rationals, $\kappa$ has distinct one-sided limits, given by the upper and lower horn maps of $e^{2\pi ip/q}z+z^2$ with conjugate phases on the two sides; we verify this at $1/3,1/4,2/5,3/7$ and conjecture it at every rational. Satellites of other components, including a primitive one, follow the parabolic germs at their roots. For $z^3+c$ the same picture holds with the cubic cusp germ.
